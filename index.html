FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most clients are small teams, startups and owner-operators who need expert help without a full-time hire.

How long does a project take?

A starter website usually takes 1–2 weeks. Dashboards take 1–3 weeks, depending on how many data sources are involved.

Can you support us after launch?

Yes. Book hours as you need them, or choose a monthly plan for updates, fixes and IT help.

Do you work remotely?

Most work is remote. On-site visits are available for IT setup and support in the local area.
